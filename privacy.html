DeePonyCap privacy

Effective: 14 October 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

DeePonyCap works on your device. We don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Collection (names, photos, shelves, wishlist, accessories) in localStorage / IndexedDB.
  • Optional parent PIN (stored as a hash on device only).
  • Preferences (theme, collector mode, haptics).
  • Camera / photo-library access only when you choose to add a photo; images stay local.
  • Haptics use the browser Vibration API locally after a user gesture (never sent to a server).

What leaves your device

Nothing for collection data, sync, or analytics. CSP connect-src 'self' — no third-party APIs.

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
GitHub Pages (static host) Normal browser request metadata for HTML/JS/CSS/icons Serve the PWA First visit and when the service worker refreshes cached assets

After first load, the app works offline via the service worker. No cloud backup unless you export a file yourself.

Your controls

Export / import / erase from Settings. Optional Parent Lock PIN can gate export, import, and delete. Clearing site data removes local copies.

Children

DeePonyCap is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. Parents can enable an optional Parent Lock PIN; supervise exports and device sharing.

Independence

DeePonyCap is an independent collection tracker and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any toy company.

Changes

We'll update this page and its effective date if anything changes.
